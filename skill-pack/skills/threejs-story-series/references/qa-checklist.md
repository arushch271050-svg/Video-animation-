# QA checklist (tick every line before delivering)

## Script / voice
- [ ] One sentence per row; phrases ≤ 7 words; numbers as words; no ranges
- [ ] Listened to the take; doubtful words checked by ear, not only by whisper
- [ ] align.py `overlaps: 0`; each caption appears WITH its words (spot-check 3 lines in the render)

## Picture (contact sheet of every cut + strips of every action)
- [ ] No camera inside a head/hair/wall; no black frames; nothing crossing between camera and subject
- [ ] Every face lit (fill light) and readable; key subject not under the captions (y 1230–1450)
- [ ] Same character = same Look + seed everywhere; props in the same places
- [ ] Action: feet don't slide, nobody walks through furniture or people, no frozen tail at the end of a shot
- [ ] Gore (only if the bible allows it) attached to the rig / continuous trails, not floating balls or rectangles
- [ ] The look: TiltShift on, a Glow on every visible practical light, every shot pushes in
- [ ] Every card that pops in has a Click; cards are few (rule, time, number, next)
- [ ] episode.json keywords updated; end card = next episode; cover = this episode, title on top

## Sound
- [ ] SFX land on the picture (onsets measured); "voice" is the aligned take; "video" is the fresh render
- [ ] −14 ±1 LUFS, peak ≤ −1 dBFS, duration = the render
- [ ] Final MP4 re-transcribed: every line intelligible over the bed

## Delivery
- [ ] MP4 + cover + episode folder + caption draft + known gaps; nothing posted without the owner
